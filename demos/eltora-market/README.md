# ELTORA MARKET
Frontend demo: HTML, CSS, vanilla JS, Three.js. Open `index.html` or serve the folder statically.

- Exactly 20 photos live in `assets/images/`. `js/products.js` (`SRC` map) points every product, category, lifestyle and banner slot at one of them, so photos are reused across slots.
- Cart and wishlist use localStorage. Delivery: Rs 40 under Rs 500, free above.
- Three.js: see `assets/3d/`.
