About ELTORA

Jewellery With A Story.

ELTORA JEWELS began as a small design studio with one belief: that fine jewellery should be worn, not just kept. Every collection is built around pieces that move with you — from a Tuesday morning to a wedding day.

We work with ethically sourced stones and recycled precious metals wherever possible, and every setting passes through the hands of a master goldsmith before it reaches you. It is a modern approach to an old craft — heritage techniques, contemporary lines.
