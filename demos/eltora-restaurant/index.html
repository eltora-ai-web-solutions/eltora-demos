Made Around The Fire.

At ELTORA, every dish begins with a simple belief — exceptional food does not need to be complicated. It needs to be thoughtful.

We buy from growers we know and cook what the season gives us. Fresh ingredients are handled lightly, then finished over open fire, where char and smoke do the work of a sauce. Each plate is put together by hand, and every guest is looked after the way a host would look after friends at home.
